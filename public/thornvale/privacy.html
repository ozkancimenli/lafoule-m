Thornvale Defense – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Thornvale Defense ("the game") is developed by Ozkan Cimenli ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The current version of Thornvale Defense does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It does not require an account, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Advertising

The current version shows no third-party ads. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before release to name the ad provider and describe what it collects, and you will be asked for permission where required (for example, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
