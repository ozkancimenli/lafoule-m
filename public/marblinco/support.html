Marblinco: Support

Marblinco is an endless runner about a little robot who escaped a factory. If something isn't working, these answers usually help.

I bought something but didn't get it

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't appear, contact us below with the date of the purchase.

How do I get more hacks?

Hacks clear a car out of your way. You get them from chips on the road, the daily mission chest, the login streak, and the Shop (with coins or as a pack).

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress, and we can't restore it, because we don't store it anywhere else.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
