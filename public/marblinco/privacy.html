Marblinco: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Marblinco is a mobile game made by Ozkan Cimenli ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information we do not collect

Marblinco has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the game.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocked looks, missions, settings and best distance) is saved only on your own device. If you delete the game, this data is deleted with it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered.

Advertising

The free version of Marblinco may show ads. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which runs an auction between ad networks, currently Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, these companies may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and general ad interaction data. If the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) and you decline, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can read how they use this data here: Unity privacy policy and Google's partner-sites policy. Buying "No Ads" turns off the ads that play between runs and makes revives free; other reward ads stay optional.

In-game advertising (billboards)

Some billboards and vehicles inside the game show the names of partner brands. These are part of the game's art; they do not collect any information about you.

Children

Marblinco is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Marblinco support page.
